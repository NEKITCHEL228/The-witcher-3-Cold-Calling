# Game Jam "Дикая неОхота" Constraints & Lore

## 1. Core Rule & Premise
> **«Герою надоело сражаться с нечистью. И он делает с ней что угодно, но не сражается (18+ запрещено!). Это главное условие для вашей игры.»**

Geralt (Геральт из Ривии) has retired his silver sword and taken a corporate cubicle job at a shady predatory credit call-center. Equipped with an awkward headset, a cracked coffee mug, and a script manual, his survival depends not on signs or potions, but on meeting his daily loan disbursement quota by selling high-interest microloans to monsters over a crackly rotary phone line.

## 2. Additional Jam Themes Integration

1. **✅ Побочный эффект (Side Effect)**:
   - Every financial product or item has an unintended consequence. For example: A stimulant coffee gives +15% persuasion luck, but accelerates monster annoyance timer; an easy loan agreement grants immediate cash but increases the monster's anger threshold in subsequent calls.

2. **✅ Не тот герой (Wrong Hero)**:
   - Geralt is the epitome of the wrong guy for customer support: gruff, laconic, emotionally monotone ("Хм... Зараза..."). His natural dialogue options clash with corporate customer service scripts, creating comedic dissonance.

3. **✅ Учитесь у своих врагов (Learn From Your Enemies)**:
   - Geralt leverages his encyclopedic knowledge of monster folklore and habits (ghouls love easy scavenged collateral, noonwraiths have existential property disputes, drowners need waterproof insurance) to manipulate their negotiations. Defeating or pitching to a monster can yield new "sales tactics" inspired by their traits.

4. **✅ Палка о двух концах (Double-Edged Sword)**:
   - High-yield aggressive dialogue choices: high persuasion payout if successful, but a high risk of instant hang-up or severe penalty on failure.

5. **✅ Проклятие знаний (Curse of Knowledge)**:
   - Overthinking or knowing too much about a monster's lore can trigger specific dialogue choices that perplex or provoke the monster if they don't appreciate being analyzed. Also manifests as a shop perk or monster trait.

## 3. Tone & Narrative Style
- Dark humor, corporate satire mixed with Slavic dark fantasy lore.
- Satirical dialogue: corporate jargon ("гибкие процентные ставки", "залоговое имущество", "реструктуризация долга") meets folklore ("крипта под залог полуденного венка").
- Strictly non-violent resolution: contracts signed, loans approved, or irritated hang-ups.
